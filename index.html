Our Story

Welcome to Glengala Fresh! We're a family-owned fresh produce shop in Sunshine, Melbourne.

Wayne and Dee hand-pick the freshest produce from Melbourne's Epping Market, and cold-press our juices fresh daily using ozonated water.

We've been serving the Sunshine community with quality fruit, veg, eggs, nuts, and cold-pressed juice — fair prices, no fuss.
